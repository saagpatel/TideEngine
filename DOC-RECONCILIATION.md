## Drifted Claims Fixed This Pass

### README.md — Architecture section (two errors in one paragraph)

**File:** `README.md:49–51`

**Evidence basis:** `verified-by-reading-code`

**Before:**
> The physics engine runs on a dedicated `Task` at 2 Hz, computing lunar and solar unit vectors
> and the tidal potential field across a 64×32 latitude/longitude grid. The Metal shader reads
> this grid as a texture and displaces the globe mesh vertices in real time.

**After:**
> The tidal heightfield recomputes at ~2 Hz (every 0.5 s) via `SCNSceneRendererDelegate`'s
> `renderer(_:updateAtTime:)` callback, computing lunar and solar unit vectors and the tidal
> potential field across a 64×32 latitude/longitude grid. A Metal compute shader upsamples this
> grid to a 512×256 color texture that drives the sphere's emission material in real time.

**Why:** Two factual errors in the original text:
1. "dedicated `Task`" — the update loop is `SCNSceneRendererDelegate.renderer(_:updateAtTime:)` with
   a `guard time - lastUpdateTime > 0.5` throttle (`GlobeView.swift:49–52`), not a Swift Task.
2. "displaces the globe mesh vertices" — the Metal shader writes colors to a 512×256 texture
   (`Shaders.metal:54`), which is applied as the sphere's `material.emission.contents`
   (`GlobeScene.swift:30`). Vertex geometry is never modified.
