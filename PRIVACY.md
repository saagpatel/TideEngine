# Privacy Policy: Tide Engine

Tide Engine does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or user accounts.

## Location

Location access is optional and requested only when you tap the arrow button (VoiceOver reads it as "Show tides near me"). Your device uses the coordinate to select a nearby NOAA tide station. Tide Engine does not send your precise device coordinate to NOAA or save it to persistent storage. The coordinate is held in memory for the globe and tide detail view. The selected NOAA station and cached tide predictions may be stored on your device and shared with the Tide Engine widget.

## Network requests

To find a station, Tide Engine requests NOAA's station list unless a valid local copy is available. Prediction requests contain the selected NOAA station identifier and date range, including when the station was selected using your location. NOAA receives ordinary network information such as your IP address under its own policies. The widget reads locally cached data and does not contact NOAA.

## Storage and sharing

Tide Engine stores the NOAA station list, last selected station, and cached tide predictions locally on your device. It does not sell personal information or share it with advertising or analytics companies.
