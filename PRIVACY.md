# Privacy Policy — Tide Engine

Tide Engine does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or user accounts.

## Location

Location access is optional and requested only when you tap the location button. Your device uses the coordinate to select a nearby NOAA tide station. Tide Engine does not send your precise device coordinate to NOAA and does not retain it. The selected NOAA station and cached tide predictions may be stored on your device and shared with the Tide Engine widget.

## Network requests

When you request tide predictions, Tide Engine contacts the U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) Tides and Currents service. Those requests contain a NOAA station identifier and date range. NOAA receives ordinary network information such as your IP address under its own policies.

## Storage and sharing

Tide Engine stores preferences and recent NOAA station data locally on your device. It does not sell personal information or share it with advertising or analytics companies.
